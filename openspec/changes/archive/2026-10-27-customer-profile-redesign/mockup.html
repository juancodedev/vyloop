A loyalty card in your pocket, not a dashboard

Redesign proposal for /profile in customer-web. It keeps Vyloop's amber and stone tokens and Inter, borrows the reference's identity-first layout, and makes the next reward the one thing you see. Proposal only: nothing here is implemented.

Why change it

The current page answers "what do I have" well (the balance is the single big number), but it never answers "what do I get, and when". The customer has to open /profile/rewards to find out. It also shows no personal data, so a customer cannot check or fix the phone or email the club uses to reach them.

The reference screen gets the order right: who you are, what you can win, what happened, your data. It also has problems worth not copying, listed below.

Take from the reference

  • Identity card with initials avatar
  • Rewards above activity
  • Timeline with a rail
  • Personal data block with Edit
  • Delete account at the end

Leave out

  • English dates ("a year ago") in a Spanish UI
  • All-caps stat labels
  • "7 puntos" next to a reward priced in Burger: two units for one thing
  • Empty fields shown as "-" (Tipo cliente)
  • A thin progress bar that hides how close the reward is

Page order

Ordered by how often a customer needs each block. The QR leaves the scroll and becomes a fixed button, because at the counter speed matters more than anything else on this page.

  1. 1Identity cardAvatar, full name, member since, tier chip, points balance, progress to next tier.
  2. 2Next redemption card ("Tu próximo canje")The signature element: a ticket with the price on a perforated stub and a progress bar. Links to the full catalog.
  3. 3Recent purchasesLast 3 to 5 purchases with date, amount and points earned, closed by how close the next reward is. "Ver todo" goes to /profile/history.
  4. 4Wallet cardToday's WalletCard, reduced to one row.
  5. 5Your dataName, phone, email, birthday. Only filled fields render.
  6. 6NotificationsConsent toggles, including nearby_notifications from the enable-nearby-consent change.
  7. 7AccountLog out, then delete account in a quiet danger color.
  8. +Dock: "Mostrar mi código"Fixed at the bottom, opens the QR sheet with the member code.

Component specs

Identity card

  • Initials from the first given name and first surname (Juan Muñoz Castillo gives JM), on --brand-100 with --brand-900 text.
  • One stat: the points balance with the program's pointsCurrency, as the page shows it today. No purchase or visit counts. "Acumulados" and "Últimos 30 días" move to /profile/history.
  • The tier chip renders only when today's rule holds (currentTierId set and a program exists). Same for the tier line; at the top tier it reads "Estás en el nivel más alto".
  • No program configured: keep today's "Tu programa se está configurando" status in place of stats and tier line.

Next redemption card

  • Heading "Tu próximo canje", with the reward's own catalog name as the title. Everything is counted in points; the reward name never becomes the unit.
  • Points are earned by purchase amount, not by visit, so the card uses a progress bar with numbers ("30 de 100 puntos") instead of stamps. A stamp reads as one visit; here one purchase can add many points.
  • The card keeps the shape of a paper ticket: the price sits on a stub behind a perforated edge.
  • Shows the cheapest active reward the customer cannot afford yet. If some reward is already affordable, the card switches to "Ya podés canjear Burger gratis" with a "Canjear" button to /profile/rewards/confirm.
  • Price, balance and remaining use the same unit: "Te faltan 70 puntos".
  • Sold-out rewards (stock === 0) are skipped when choosing.

Recent purchases

  • One row per purchase: amount spent as the title, date below, points earned and resulting balance on the right. No item detail.
  • Redemptions show as their own rows ("Canjeaste Burger gratis", "−100 puntos"). Text carries the direction, never color or glyph alone, as today.
  • The list closes with one line tying it to the goal: "Con 70 puntos más, tu canje Burger gratis es tuyo". It disappears when no reward is in the catalog.
  • Dates are relative up to 7 days ("Hace 2 días"), then short absolute ("28 sep").
  • Rows without a purchase amount (manual adjustments, welcome bonus) fall back to their note, so nothing shows "$0".

Your data

  • Read-only list. "Editar" opens a form for name, email and birthday. Phone is the login identity and stays read-only, with a hint to contact the club to change it.
  • Birthday shows day and month only; the year is not useful to show back.

QR sheet

  • Bottom sheet over a dark scrim, QR at 210px minimum, member code in groups of three for dictation.
  • Requests a screen wake lock while open and tolerates refusal.
  • When the member code has not been minted yet, the sheet shows only the QR and says the code appears after adding the card to Wallet, matching today's WalletCard behavior.

States

Loading

Skeleton of the new order: identity card, reward card, three purchase rows. No spinner.

No activity

Todavía no hay movimientos. Cuando sumes en el local, aparecen acá.

No rewards in catalog

The stamp card is hidden. No placeholder reward is invented.

Error

Today's ClubError with "Reintentar". No fallback profile.

Suggested slicing

  1. Frontend only: new order, identity card without visits, timeline, dock and QR sheet, read-only data.
  2. Backend nextReward and purchaseAmount, then the redemption card and purchase rows.
  3. Edit data with PATCH /v1/customers/me.
  4. Delete account, after retention rules are agreed.

Acceptance criteria

  • The QR is reachable in one tap from any scroll position.
  • Balance, reward price and points missing all use the program's pointsCurrency. A reward's name is never used as a unit.
  • No tier, reward or code is shown unless the API returned it.
  • All dates are in Spanish (es-CL), relative up to 7 days and absolute after.
  • Empty personal fields are hidden, not shown as "-".
  • Existing behavior tests in app/profile/*.test.tsx keep passing or are updated for the new order with the same assertions on data honesty.
